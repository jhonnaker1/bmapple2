KEGS
====

BMApple2's emulation is KEGS 1.38 (https://kegs.sourceforge.net/), the Apple
IIgs emulator by Kent Dickey, built into the kernels with BMApple2's
platform layer (src/port/). Its notice, as in its source files:

    KEGS: Apple //gs Emulator

The GNU General Public License version 3 is LICENSE.txt in the root of the
card. BMApple2's menu uses the Apple II character set from KEGS's font.

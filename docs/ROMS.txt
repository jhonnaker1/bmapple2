Apple IIgs and Apple IIe ROMs
=============================

BMApple2 needs the ROM of the machine it emulates. It is copyrighted by
Apple, so it is not included: copy your own ROM image into this folder.

Apple IIgs (the usual choice)
  ROM.03         ROM 3, 256K (also found as APPLE2GS.ROM2 or Rom03gd)
  ROM.01         ROM 01, 128K (also found as APPLE2GS.ROM, xgs.rom or
                 342-0077-b)

Apple IIe
  APPLE2E.ROM    an enhanced IIe ROM in the 32K form other emulators use
                 (the IIe's 16K ROM in the last 16K, the Disk II card's PROM
                 at $600)

If there are several, BMApple2 starts the first it finds: ROM 3, then
ROM 01, then the IIe. Choose another with F4 (KEGS's settings), ROM File
Selection.

From a MAME ROM set (also Ample's on the Mac)
---------------------------------------------
MAME keeps the ROM chips, not ROM files. Copy these files out of the zip
files into this folder as they are, and BMApple2 makes ROM.03 and
APPLE2E.ROM from them the first time it starts:

  Apple IIgs ROM 3   apple2gs.zip:    341-0728 and 341-0748
  Apple IIgs ROM 01  apple2gs.zip:    apple2gsr1/342-0077-b (used as it is)
  Apple IIe          apple2e.zip:     apple2ee/342-0304-a.e10 and
                                      apple2ee/342-0303-a.e8 (enhanced),
                                      or 342-0135-b.64 and 342-0134-a.64
                     a2diskiing.zip:  341-0027-a.p5 (the Disk II card)

Ample keeps its ROM set in ~/Library/Application Support/Ample/roms.
